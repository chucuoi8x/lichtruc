The lunar-year data in the offline tool is extracted from the author's
amlich-hnd.js (1800–2199). The tool decodes those tables without a network
connection or dependency on the computer's timezone.

Source: https://www.xemamlich.uhm.vn/JavaScript/amlich-hnd.js
Documentation: https://www.xemamlich.uhm.vn/JavaScript/
Original author: Hồ Ngọc Đức.

The scheduling, local data management and export UI are separate application code.
